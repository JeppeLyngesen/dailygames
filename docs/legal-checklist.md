# Legal & compliance checklist

Not legal advice: a starting point. Check the official sources linked.

## Now (phase 1)
- [x] "Not affiliated" notice in footer + About page
- [x] Game names used only to describe/link; no logos or copied artwork
- [x] No cookies or third-party scripts; data stays in the user's browser (privacy page says so)
- [ ] Contact e-mail on About page
- [ ] Donations (Buy Me a Coffee, buymeacoffee.com/premoderncollection): income is taxable for you personally – keep a record (skat.dk)

## Before adding analytics
- [ ] Cookieless tool (Cloudflare Web Analytics) → update privacy page; no consent banner needed for truly cookieless, anonymous stats, but re-check Datatilsynet guidance

## Before adding ads (phase 3)
- [ ] AdSense account approved (needs original, useful content and a privacy policy)
- [ ] Google-certified consent banner (CMP) for EEA/UK users – AdSense offers a free one under "Privacy & messaging"
- [ ] "Reject all" as easy as "Accept all"; no ads/cookies load before a choice
- [ ] Privacy page lists Google as a partner and links to its policies
- [ ] ads.txt file at the domain root (requires custom domain)

## Before accounts (phase 3)
- [ ] Privacy policy: what personal data, why, how long, how to delete (GDPR art. 13)
- [ ] "Delete my account" function
- [ ] Data processor agreement with hosting provider (Azure has a standard DPA)

## Business
- [ ] Register CVR (enkeltmandsvirksomhed, virk.dk) once income is regular; ask SKAT about the "hobby vs business" line
- [ ] Simple bookkeeping (free tier of a Danish bookkeeping app, or a spreadsheet at first)
